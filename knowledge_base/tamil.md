# Tamil Knowledge Base

These are English medium support notes for Tamil language and literature. They
help the bot explain answer structure, grammar, meaning, and literature analysis
without replacing Tamil textbooks or teacher notes.

## Exam Answer Pattern

For Tamil language questions, identify the grammar rule, explain meaning or
usage, and give a correct example. For literature questions, mention the text,
context, character, feeling, and message.

## Language Skills

Good Tamil answers use accurate vocabulary, correct spelling, suitable sentence
order, and proper punctuation. Vocabulary questions should include meaning,
context, and a simple example.

Grammar topics may include word classes, tense, number, gender, case, sentence
patterns, idioms, proverbs, and transformations. Keep examples short and clear.

## Literature Skills

For poems, stories, drama, and prose, identify the speaker or character, the
situation, the main idea, and the emotional effect. Explain how words and
images create meaning.

Character answers should mention actions, speech, qualities, relationships,
and change. Theme answers should connect events to values, society, family,
nature, courage, duty, or justice.

## Writing Skills

Essays need introduction, ordered points, examples, and conclusion. Letters
must follow formal or informal format. Summaries should include only main ideas.

## Common Problem Templates

Grammar question:
State the rule, apply it to the sentence, and provide the corrected form.

Literature question:
Name the text, describe the situation, explain meaning, and add the message.

Writing task:
Plan first, organize paragraphs, use suitable words, and check accuracy.
