# Science Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Science answers should state the scientific idea, explain the process or reason,
and give an observation, example, equation, or diagram label when useful.

## Biology

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Plant
cells usually have a cell wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole, nucleus, cytoplasm,
and cell membrane. Animal cells have nucleus, cytoplasm, and cell membrane but
do not have chloroplasts or a cellulose cell wall.

Photosynthesis is the process where green plants make glucose using carbon
dioxide and water in the presence of light and chlorophyll. Oxygen is released
as a by-product. The word equation is: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose +
oxygen.

Respiration releases energy from food. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and
produces carbon dioxide, water, and energy. Anaerobic respiration happens
without oxygen and releases less energy.

Human digestion breaks food into soluble substances that can be absorbed.
Starch digestion begins in the mouth. Proteins are mainly digested in the
stomach and small intestine. The small intestine absorbs digested food.

Blood transports oxygen, carbon dioxide, nutrients, hormones, and waste.
Red blood cells carry oxygen, white blood cells defend against disease,
platelets help clotting, and plasma carries dissolved substances.

## Chemistry

Matter exists as solids, liquids, and gases. Solids have fixed shape and volume.
Liquids have fixed volume but no fixed shape. Gases have no fixed shape or
volume and can be compressed.

Atoms are the smallest particles of elements that take part in chemical
reactions. Molecules are groups of atoms bonded together. Compounds contain
two or more elements chemically combined.

Acids turn blue litmus red and have pH below 7. Alkalis turn red litmus blue
and have pH above 7. Neutral solutions have pH 7. Neutralization forms salt
and water.

Metals usually conduct heat and electricity, are malleable, ductile, and shiny.
Non-metals are often poor conductors and may be brittle when solid.

Rusting needs iron, oxygen, and water. It can be prevented by painting,
oiling, galvanizing, alloying, or keeping iron dry.

## Physics

Force can change the motion, speed, direction, or shape of an object. The SI
unit of force is newton. Weight is the gravitational force on a mass.

Speed = distance / time. Acceleration = change in velocity / time. A distance-
time graph shows speed by its gradient.

Energy is the ability to do work. Common forms include kinetic, potential,
thermal, chemical, electrical, light, and sound energy. Energy can be changed
from one form to another but is conserved overall.

Electric current is the flow of charge. Voltage is the energy transferred per
unit charge. Resistance opposes current. Ohm's law: V = IR.

Series circuits have one path for current. Parallel circuits have separate
branches. In a series circuit current is the same through each component. In a
parallel circuit voltage is the same across each branch.

## Environment And Earth Science

Ecosystems contain living components and non-living components. Food chains
show energy flow from producers to consumers. Decomposers recycle nutrients.

Pollution can affect air, water, and soil. Control methods include reducing
waste, treating sewage, using cleaner energy, recycling, and protecting forests.

## Common Problem Templates

Experiment question:
State aim, apparatus, method, variables, observations, conclusion, and safety.

Calculation question:
Write formula, substitute values with units, calculate, and state final unit.

Process question:
Write steps in correct order and connect each step with a reason or result.
