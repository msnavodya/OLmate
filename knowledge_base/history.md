# History Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

History answers should show time order, causes, important people or places,
events, and effects. Use dates when you know them, but explain why events
matter instead of listing facts only.

## Core Skills

Chronology means arranging events in the order they happened. Cause means why
an event happened. Effect means the result or change that followed. Evidence
means facts, sources, objects, buildings, inscriptions, records, or accounts
used to understand the past.

## Answer Structures

Cause question:
Write the background, list main causes, explain each cause, and identify the
immediate trigger if there is one.

Effect question:
Write the event first, then explain political, economic, social, cultural, or
religious effects.

Compare question:
Use the same categories for both sides. For example leadership, economy,
religion, foreign relations, and society.

Source question:
Identify what the source shows, who made it if known, what it tells us, and
what its limitation may be.

## Sri Lankan History Revision Themes

Ancient kingdoms developed around irrigation, agriculture, trade, religion,
and strong administration. Tanks, canals, stupas, inscriptions, and ruins are
important evidence for studying ancient Sri Lanka.

Irrigation supported agriculture by storing and distributing water. Kings who
developed irrigation strengthened food production and settlement.

Buddhism influenced education, art, architecture, literature, and moral values
in Sri Lankan history. Temples were also centres of learning and culture.

Foreign contacts affected trade, religion, language, administration, and
technology. Coastal location made Sri Lanka important in Indian Ocean trade.

Colonial rule changed administration, economy, education, law, transport, and
social life. Plantation agriculture, roads, railways, and ports shaped the
modern economy.

## World History Revision Themes

Civilizations develop near rivers because water supports farming, transport,
settlement, and trade.

Wars often have long-term causes, immediate causes, major events, and effects.
A strong answer separates these parts clearly.

Industrial development changes production, transport, cities, labour, and
living conditions.

## Common Problem Templates

Why did this happen:
Use political, economic, social, and immediate causes.

What were the results:
Use short-term results and long-term effects.

Importance question:
Explain how the person, event, or place changed society or influenced later
events.
