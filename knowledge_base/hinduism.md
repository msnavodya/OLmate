# Hinduism Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Hinduism answers should state the teaching, practice, or value, explain its
meaning, and connect it to daily life, duty, respect, and self-discipline.

## Core Ideas

Hinduism emphasizes dharma, devotion, duty, self-control, respect, truthfulness,
non-violence, purity, and service. Religious practice helps a person live with
discipline and spiritual awareness.

Dharma means righteous duty or right conduct. It guides people to act properly
as children, students, family members, and citizens.

Karma teaches that actions have results. Good actions bring good effects and
wrong actions create suffering. This encourages responsibility.

Worship, prayer, festivals, charity, respect for elders, and service are
important parts of religious life.

## Life Application

A student can apply Hindu values by respecting parents and teachers, studying
diligently, speaking truthfully, helping others, controlling anger, and caring
for living beings.

Festivals strengthen devotion, family unity, culture, generosity, and moral
reflection.

## Common Problem Templates

Teaching question:
Name the teaching, explain it, and give a daily-life example.

Festival question:
Explain the religious meaning, practices, values, and social importance.

Value question:
State the value, show correct behaviour, and explain its benefit.
