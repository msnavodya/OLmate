# Islam Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Islam answers should state the belief, practice, or value, explain its meaning,
and connect it to disciplined and righteous daily life.

## Core Beliefs And Practices

Islam teaches belief in one God, accountability, moral conduct, compassion,
justice, and obedience to divine guidance.

The Five Pillars guide Muslim life: declaration of faith, prayer, charity,
fasting in Ramadan, and pilgrimage for those who are able.

Prayer builds discipline, remembrance of God, cleanliness, humility, and unity.
Charity reduces selfishness and supports people in need. Fasting teaches self-
control, patience, gratitude, and concern for the poor.

Honesty, respect for parents, kindness to neighbours, justice, modesty, and
care for the environment are important values.

## Life Application

A student can apply Islamic values by being truthful, keeping promises,
respecting parents and teachers, helping others, avoiding waste, and controlling
anger.

Community life is strengthened by charity, forgiveness, cooperation, and fair
treatment of others.

## Common Problem Templates

Practice question:
Name the practice, explain its purpose, and give a life benefit.

Value question:
State the value, give an example, and explain how it improves society.

Belief question:
Explain the belief simply and connect it to responsibility and conduct.
