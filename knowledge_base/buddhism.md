# Buddhism Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Buddhism answers should state the teaching, explain its meaning, give an
example from life or Buddhist tradition, and show how it guides good conduct.

## Core Teachings

The Buddha taught the importance of wisdom, morality, mental discipline, and
compassion. Buddhist practice encourages avoiding evil, doing good, and
purifying the mind.

The Four Noble Truths explain suffering, its cause, its ending, and the path
leading to its ending. Craving is a main cause of suffering. The Noble Eightfold
Path guides right understanding, thought, speech, action, livelihood, effort,
mindfulness, and concentration.

The Five Precepts guide moral life: avoid killing, stealing, sexual misconduct,
false speech, and intoxicants. They protect self and society.

Kamma means intentional action. Good actions lead to beneficial results, while
bad actions bring harmful results. This teaching encourages responsibility.

Compassion, loving-kindness, patience, generosity, truthfulness, and respect
are important Buddhist values.

## Life Application

Right speech means speaking truthfully, kindly, usefully, and at the right time.
It helps reduce conflict at home, school, and society.

Mindfulness helps students control anger, reduce carelessness, and improve
learning. Generosity reduces selfishness and builds friendship.

## Common Problem Templates

Teaching explanation:
Name the teaching, explain it simply, and give a daily-life example.

Value question:
State the value, describe good behaviour, and explain the benefit to society.

Story question:
Identify the event, explain the lesson, and connect it to modern life.
