# Sinhala Knowledge Base

These are English medium support notes for Sinhala language and literature.
They help the bot explain answer structure, grammar, meaning, and literature
analysis without replacing Sinhala textbooks or teacher notes.

## Exam Answer Pattern

For Sinhala language questions, identify the grammar point, explain the meaning
or usage, and give a correct example. For literature questions, mention the
text, situation, character, feeling, and message.

## Language Skills

A strong Sinhala answer should use suitable vocabulary, correct sentence order,
accurate spelling, and punctuation. When explaining a word, include meaning,
context, and a sample sentence.

Grammar answers often ask about word classes, tense, gender, number, case,
sentence structure, idioms, proverbs, and transformations. Always keep examples
short and correct.

## Literature Skills

When answering about a poem, story, drama, or prose text, identify the speaker
or character, the situation, the main idea, and the emotional effect. Explain
how language creates meaning.

Character questions should include personality traits, actions, speech,
relationships, and changes in the story.

Theme questions should connect the text to a central idea such as duty, love,
patriotism, social values, nature, conflict, courage, or moral responsibility.

## Writing Skills

Essay writing should have a clear beginning, ordered ideas, examples, and a
conclusion. Letter writing should match the required formal or informal format.
Summary writing should keep only the main ideas and avoid unnecessary details.

## Common Problem Templates

Meaning question:
Give the simple meaning, explain the context, and write a correct sentence.

Literature appreciation:
Name the text, describe the situation, explain the idea, and comment on the
language or message.

Essay:
Plan points first, write connected paragraphs, and end with a direct conclusion.
