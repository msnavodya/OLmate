# Geography Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Geography answers should mention place, process, factors, evidence, and impact.
For map questions, use direction, distance, scale, symbols, grid references,
and physical or human features.

## Map Skills

A map represents an area using symbols and scale. Scale shows the relationship
between map distance and real distance. Direction is commonly shown by north
arrow or compass points.

Contour lines join points of equal height. Close contours show steep slopes.
Wide contours show gentle slopes. Rivers usually flow from higher land to lower
land.

Grid references help locate places. Read eastings first and northings second.

## Physical Geography

Weather is the condition of the atmosphere at a particular time and place.
Climate is the average weather pattern of a place over a long period.

Rainfall can be convectional, relief, or cyclonic. Relief rainfall occurs when
moist air rises over high land, cools, condenses, and forms rain.

Rivers erode, transport, and deposit materials. Upper course features include
steep slopes and waterfalls. Lower course features include meanders, flood
plains, and deltas.

Soil, climate, relief, water, and vegetation affect agriculture and settlement.

## Human Geography

Population distribution is affected by climate, relief, water, jobs, transport,
soil, safety, and services. Urbanization means growth of towns and cities.

Industries are located based on raw materials, labour, power, transport, market,
capital, and government policy.

Transport and communication help trade, migration, education, services, and
national development.

## Environment

Environmental problems include deforestation, soil erosion, pollution, floods,
landslides, and waste. Solutions include conservation, reforestation, proper
land use, recycling, disaster preparedness, and sustainable resource use.

## Common Problem Templates

Map question:
Identify symbol or feature, use direction and distance, and support with map
evidence.

Process question:
Write the steps in order and explain the conditions needed.

Factor question:
Group factors as physical, human, economic, and political where possible.
