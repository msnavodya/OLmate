# Civic Education Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Civic Education answers should define the concept, explain citizen responsibility,
give examples from school, family, community, or country, and show why it helps
society.

## Citizenship

A citizen is a member of a state who has rights and responsibilities. Good
citizens obey the law, respect others, protect public property, vote when
eligible, help the community, and act honestly.

Rights are freedoms or entitlements people should enjoy. Responsibilities are
duties people should perform. Rights and responsibilities must be balanced.

## Democracy And Governance

Democracy is government by the people, usually through elected representatives.
Important democratic values include equality, freedom, participation, rule of
law, accountability, tolerance, and respect for majority and minority views.

The rule of law means everyone, including leaders, must obey the law. It
protects fairness and prevents misuse of power.

Public institutions provide services and maintain order. Citizens should use
public services responsibly and protect public resources.

## Society And Values

Family, school, religious institutions, media, and community organizations help
socialization. Social harmony needs respect, cooperation, empathy, fairness,
and non-violent conflict resolution.

Diversity means differences in religion, language, ethnicity, culture, ability,
and opinion. Respect for diversity helps national unity.

## Law And Peace

Laws guide behaviour and protect people. Conflict should be solved through
dialogue, mediation, compromise, and lawful methods.

## Common Problem Templates

Rights question:
State the right, explain its importance, and mention a matching responsibility.

Democracy question:
Define democracy, give features, and explain benefits to citizens.

Social problem question:
Describe the problem, causes, effects, and practical solutions.
