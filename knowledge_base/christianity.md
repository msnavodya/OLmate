# Christianity Knowledge Base

## Exam Answer Pattern

Christianity answers should state the teaching or event, explain its meaning,
connect it to Christian values, and give a practical life application.

## Core Teachings

Christian faith emphasizes love of God and love of neighbour. Important values
include faith, hope, charity, forgiveness, humility, service, honesty, justice,
and peace.

Jesus taught people to love others, forgive enemies, care for the poor, act
with mercy, and live truthfully. Parables teach moral and spiritual lessons
through simple stories.

Prayer, worship, reading scripture, service, and moral living help Christians
grow in faith.

Forgiveness is central to Christian conduct. It does not mean approving wrong,
but it helps overcome hatred and restore relationships.

## Life Application

A student can apply Christian values by helping classmates, speaking honestly,
respecting parents and teachers, avoiding revenge, and serving people in need.

Love and service build peace in family, school, church, and society. Humility
helps a person accept correction and treat others with respect.

## Common Problem Templates

Teaching question:
State the teaching, explain the value, and give an example from daily life.

Parable question:
Name the parable, describe the situation, identify the lesson, and apply it.

Value question:
Explain the value and show how it improves relationships and society.
